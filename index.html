Session settings & assumptions

London 08:00–11:00 and NY 14:00–17:00 are editable watch windows, NOT verified Bared FX trade hours. NY open / late NY boundaries are unknown, so NY is always marked caution until you define your own rules. Asia 22:00–01:00 is blocked. Stockholm time automatically adjusts for daylight saving.
